THE ADVENTURES OF SPARKY AND LEO
================================

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (e.g. your Desktop). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click SparkyLeo.exe.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway"
     (it's a home-made game, so it isn't signed).
   - If it complains about missing DLLs or DirectX, run Engine\Extras\Redist\en-us\UEPrereqSetup_x64.exe
     once, then try again.
3. Alt+Enter switches between a window and fullscreen.

CONTROLS
                 Keyboard + mouse        Keyboard only*          Xbox-style controller
  Move           WASD / arrows           WASD                    Left stick
  Jump           Space (again: flip)     Space                   A
  Swipe / talk   J, F or left click      J or F                  X
  Ground pound   Shift in the air        Shift in the air        LT / RT in the air
  Camera         Mouse, Q/E, wheel       Arrows, Q/E, I/O zoom   Right stick
  Recentre       Tab or L                Tab or L                LB / RB
  Pause          Enter / Esc             Enter / Esc             Start

  * Choose CONTROLS: KEYBOARD ONLY on the title screen or pause menu.

Tips: jump at the fuzzy pink velcro walls to stick to them, land on enemies' heads, and
Sparky has nine lives. He's a cat.
